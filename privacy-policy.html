Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Welcome to Wahat Al-Adhkar ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details how our iOS application handles user information and affirms our strict standard: we do not collect, harvest, store, or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details (such as names, emails, phone numbers, or credit cards) to use the app.
  • No User Tracking: The app does not track your identity across apps or websites.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The application does not contain advertising SDKs, data analytics brokers, or commercial behavioral trackers.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

To provide core Islamic features, the app may request specific system permissions. All data processed by these permissions remains strictly on your device:

  • Location: Used exclusively on your device to calculate precise astronomical prayer times based on your local coordinates and to align the Qibla compass toward the Kaaba. Location coordinates are processed locally and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Notifications: Used strictly to deliver local prayer time alerts (Adhan) and your chosen daily Adhkar reminders. You can modify or turn off notifications anytime in settings.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (Optional): Used exclusively for the interactive Quran voice recitation testing feature ("التسميع الصوتي"). Audio is analyzed locally or through standard iOS speech frameworks to verify recitation. We never record, transmit, or store your voice.

3. Local Device Storage

The application stores settings locally on your device (such as reading bookmarks in the Quran, favorite adhkar, chosen font preferences, and masbha session history). This data never leaves your device and is removed when the app is deleted.

4. Children's Privacy

Wahat Al-Adhkar does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is completely safe for families and users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

خلاصة سياسة الخصوصية بالعربية: تطبيق "واحة الأذكار" يحترم خصوصيتك بالكامل. التطبيق لا يجمع أي بيانات شخصية، ولا يطلب تسجيل دخول، ولا يشارك أي معلومات مع أي طرف ثالث. الصلاحيات المطلوبة (مثل الموقع لحساب مواقيت الصلاة والقبلة، أو الميكروفون للتسميع) تتم معالجتها بالكامل داخل جهازك دون إرسالها إلى أي خوادم خارجية.
